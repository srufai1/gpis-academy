## Mastery check

Close the lesson. Open a new terminal. Answer aloud or on paper, then test yourself by
running the commands.

1. From your home directory, go to `~/academy-labs/m1-p1/office/reports/term-2` using one
   relative path. Come back using `..` only.
2. Make a directory that you can enter but whose list of names you cannot read. Which
   permission number is that? What happens with `ls`? With `cat` on a file inside whose name
   you know?
3. Make a file, make a link to it, then move the file. Predict what `ls -l` and `cat` will
   say for the link **before** you run them.
4. A file must be read by you and your group, and changed by you only. Give the number, and
   the `chmod` command in letter form (`u`, `g`, `o`).
5. Explain to a new clerk, in two sentences, why a folder can show its files' names and still
   refuse to open them.

**You have mastered this unit when** you answered all five without help and your predictions
in 2 and 3 were right. If not, redo the guided example tomorrow, not today, and try again.
